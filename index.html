Conference papers

Morphology-Aware Nested Character Embeddings for Word Representation

Gruhesh Sri Sai Karthik Kurra, Chandanasree Moparthi, Prathipati Manish Chowdary, Pavan Kumar Pagadala

IEEE ICCCMLA 2025 · Published in IEEE Xplore

Every word is built from its characters by a character-level Transformer, so related forms share most of their computation and unseen words still get a vector. In a seeded re-implementation it beats word embeddings by 14.9 F1 on German NER (63.0 vs 48.2) and matches them on English topic classification.

Dual-Stream Artifact Detection with Iterative Evidence Refinement for Frame-Level Deepfake Recognition

Gruhesh Sri Sai Karthik Kurra, Pavan Kumar Pagadala, Malathy Batumalay, Veda Boddapati

IEEE NEPCON 2026 · Accepted, presentation December 2026

A ViT-Small detector with an edge stream for blending boundaries and a frequency stream for generator fingerprints, fused by iterative cross-attention. After an audit found that most WildDeepfake test frames came from training videos, it reports 96.3% AUC on a video-disjoint test subset (87.1% on Celeb-DF).

Prism Tuning: Repulsion-Trained Seed Embeddings in a Frozen Transformer for Non-Redundant Generation

Gruhesh Sri Sai Karthik Kurra, Pavan Kumar Pagadala, Malathy Batumalay, Sushma Reddy Koduru

IEEE NEPCON 2026 · Accepted, presentation December 2026

Only K seed vectors are trained, added to the input embeddings of a frozen transformer and pushed apart by a repulsion loss, so one prompt yields K different answers in a single batched pass with no filtering. The paper gives the method and formulation. Follow-up (Oct 2026): a redesigned version, tested in pre-registered GPU experiments on Qwen2.5, did not significantly beat tuned min-p sampling; the main suspected cause is the training data.

Preprints

BIE: Bit-Index Encoding of Sparse Neural Network Weights

Gruhesh Sri Sai Karthik Kurra

Zenodo preprint · Version 2, October 2026

A storage format for pruned weights: the gaps between non-zeros are Golomb–Rice coded, close to their entropy limit, and values are 8-bit codes stored as bitplanes. At 95% sparsity it is 44.5× smaller than float32 at 0.6% error, against 25.7× for CSR with the same values.
